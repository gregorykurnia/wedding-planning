# D, Veil (the minimal one)
**Idea:** like opening an invitation. One figure (what's still left to pay), one gold thread showing how much is paid, and only the very next payment, with a button to record it. Everything else sits behind four quiet rows you tap into.
**Moving around:** no menu on screen. The couple's names sit top left and a "Menu" button opens a full-page index of every section. "Add booking" floats in the corner.
**Density:** very low. One thing at a time, lots of air, centred like stationery.
**Type:** Cormorant Garamond (light, large) for the figure and headings; Jost for small text.
**Colour:** white lit by a soft blush glow and a hint of champagne, rose for the one thing that needs you, champagne gold for progress, warm plum-grey ink.
