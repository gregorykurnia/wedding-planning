# F, Atelier (the fuller one)
**Idea:** a bridal-atelier or stationery-suite feel. The memorable part is an arch, like a chapel window, that fills with blue from the bottom as you pay. Beside it are the next five payments on date tiles and who pays, with the full bookings table below.
**Moving around:** a side menu grouped into "Plan" and "Keep", with a G&B monogram in an arch and the countdown at its foot. On phone, a sticky top bar with a menu button.
**Density:** fullest of the three. Everything about money is on one page, but arranged calmly.
**Type:** Bodoni Moda for headings, figures and dates; Hanken Grotesk for everything else.
**Colour:** white and pale sky, deep cornflower ink, cornflower for paid ("something blue"), marigold for soon and the countdown, rose for late.
