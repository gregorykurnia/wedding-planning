# B, Stubs
**Idea:** money as tickets. Every booking is a ticket with a perforated stub showing what's left. Instalments hang off it as smaller tear-off stubs and get stamped "Paid".
**Moving around:** coloured folder tabs along the top, one sorbet colour per part of the planner. The open tab becomes the sheet you're working on. On phone the tabs scroll sideways.
**Density:** low and spacious. One huge "still to pay" figure, three tickets to pay next, then the bookings, with who pays alongside.
**Type:** Kalnia, a flamboyant high-contrast serif, for headings and money; Schibsted Grotesk for everything else.
**Colour:** a bright sorbet set (mint for Budget, plus lilac, peach, lemon, sky, pink, lime and aqua), plum ink, white tickets and tomato for late.
