# E, Garden (the medium one)
**Idea:** fresh daylight and greenery. A soft sage panel holds what's left to pay (with the paid part split by funder) next to the next four payments. Who pays and the biggest bookings follow underneath. A small eucalyptus crest frames your names.
**Moving around:** your names centred at the top with a row of section names beneath, like a wedding website. On phone, a bottom bar with labels.
**Density:** medium. The key figures up front, then the four funders and a short list of bookings with "See all".
**Type:** Plus Jakarta Sans throughout (a typeface designed for Jakarta), in light weights for figures and firmer weights for labels.
**Colour:** white and sage, leaf green for actions, peony pink for late, sun yellow for soon, sky blue for Greg's family.
