Rules

  • One mark everywhere: the knot. Don't add the names to the icon; the label under it carries them.
  • The mark is gold ink, never rose: rose means something needs you, and an icon never does.
  • Export PNGs from the SVGs for the places that don't take SVG: 180 × 180 for iPhone, 192 and 512 for Android (plus the maskable 512), and a 32 × 32 favicon fallback.
  • The opening screen is white with the mark, so the step into the white page is calm.
  • The tab name follows the top bar: it changes when you change your names in Wedding settings, and it never shows names before signing in.
