# C, Runway
**Idea:** time first. A runway runs from today to the wedding day with every payment standing on its due date, sized by amount. The next six weeks show each payment; later months are summed into one bar.
**Moving around:** a floating dark menu bar at the bottom, plus search everywhere (⌘K). On phone the runway turns vertical and the menu bar becomes five icons.
**Density:** highest of the three. A row of totals, the runway, then a full sortable bookings table with expandable parts, and who pays alongside.
**Type:** Geologica in light-to-medium weights, quiet and precise, with tabular figures.
**Colour:** white and soft mist, deep teal ink, lagoon green for paid, tangerine for overdue and soon, lilac for what-if, and a sunflower dot for the day itself.
