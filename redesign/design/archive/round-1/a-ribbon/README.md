# A, Ribbon
**Idea:** the whole budget is one ribbon. Each booking is a block sized by its price, solid where it's paid and striped where it isn't, and coloured by who pays. Each funder keeps that colour everywhere in the planner.
**Moving around:** a fixed side menu on desktop with the countdown at its foot; a floating bottom tab bar on phone.
**Density:** medium. A one-sentence summary, the ribbon with per-funder totals underneath, then "Pay next" next to a bookings table whose open row shows its instalments.
**Type:** Archivo throughout. The extra-wide cut is for headings and figures, and the normal width is for reading.
**Colour:** white and a cool wash, deep indigo ink, one bright colour per funder (cobalt, pink, violet, grey for unassigned), tomato for late and amber for soon.
